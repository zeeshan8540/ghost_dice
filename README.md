## Complete Breakdown of Game Modes

Ghost Dice features two distinct, fully realized game modes tailored for both individual competitive play and cooperative team strategy.

### 1. Classic Mode (Free-For-All)
Classic Mode is the timeless, high-stakes battle for board dominance.
- Player Count: Supports 2, 3, or 4 players.
- Colors: Players are assigned one of four iconic corner bases: Red, Green, Yellow, or Blue.
- Objective: Each player begins with 4 tokens resting in their corner yard. Your goal is to navigate all 4 tokens completely around the perimeter track, enter your colored home lane, and reach the center home triangle.
- Victory Condition: The very first player to successfully maneuver all 4 of their tokens into the center home wins the match.
- Dynamics: In Classic Mode, it is every player for themselves. Any opponent standing in your way is a target, and every square on the track is contested.

### 2. Team Up Mode (2 vs 2 Tactical Co-op)
Team Up Mode transforms the individual race into a cooperative board battle of alliances.
- Alliances: Players are organized into two competing two-player squads:
  - Team 1: Player 1 (Red) and Player 3 (Yellow)
  - Team 2: Player 2 (Green) and Player 4 (Blue)
- Shared Strategy: Teammates never capture each other. Instead, they coordinate their movements to form defensive shields, trap enemy tokens, and execute coordinated double captures.
- Total Team Victory: An individual cannot win alone. A team only claims victory when all 8 tokens across both teammates have reached the center home!
- Post-Finish Team Play: When one teammate finishes all 4 of their tokens, they do not sit out. On their turns, they continue rolling their dice and move their partner remaining tokens across the board until the entire squad crosses the finish line.

## How Everything Works in the App

Ghost Dice is designed to be intuitive, responsive, and immediate. Here is how a complete match plays out from start to finish:

### 1. Match Setup and Player Customization
From the main menu, tap either New Game (Classic) or Team Up (2 vs 2). On the setup screen, you can:
- Enter custom player names so everyone at the table sees their own name displayed on their dock.
- Review and toggle specific house rules (such as yard exit rules, three-sixes forfeiture, and exact finish requirements).
- Tap Start Game to enter the tabletop arena.

### 2. The Tabletop Arena Layout
The board is designed to mirror a real tabletop board viewed from above:
- Central 15x15 Ludo Board: Fills nearly the entire screen width, featuring vibrant colored yards, track squares, safe stars, and home triangles.
- Compact Player Docks: Four sleek corner docks frame the board, showing each player circular avatar pin, name, team indicator, and personal dice slot.
- Active Player Indicator: The player whose turn it is is prominently highlighted with a glowing gold border and an animated arrow pointing directly to their dice slot.
- Bottom Dice Selection Dock: A dedicated input panel at the bottom of the screen displays six crisp die faces (1 through 6) designed for effortless one-tap input.

### 3. Taking a Turn and Rolling
1. When your dock lights up, grab your physical die and roll it on the table in plain view of your fellow players.
2. Look at the rolled value (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6).
3. Tap the corresponding die button on the bottom panel.
4. Alternatively: If you do not have a physical die on hand, you can tap your glowing corner dock to roll a virtual die directly within the app.

### 4. Smart Legal Move Highlighting
As soon as a number is entered, Ghost Dice rules engine computes every valid move:
- Only tokens that can legally make a move will gently pulse with a highlight on the board.
- Invalid tokens remain unhighlighted, preventing illegal moves before they can happen.
- If you have multiple legal options, tap the specific token you wish to advance.
- Watch your token hop smoothly along each individual square with fluid physics, vertical arc jumps, realistic squash-and-stretch animations, and satisfying sound effects.

### 5. Smart Auto-Play (Fast-Paced Action)
In Ludo, there are many turns where only a single piece can move (for example, when you roll a 4 and only have one piece active on the track). Rather than forcing you to tap the board unnecessarily, Ghost Dice automatically moves that single piece after a brief 350-millisecond pause. This keeps games fast, fluid, and exciting without unnecessary tapping.

### 6. The 3-Second Center Roll Fairness Popup
To guarantee complete honesty and prevent accidental or intentional misclicks:
- Every time a player enters a number and moves a piece, a large, beautifully rendered 3D die displaying the chosen number pops up directly in the center home box of the board for 3 seconds.
- The popup displays the player name, their team, and the exact rolled number.
- Every player sitting around the table can see, confirm, and verify the roll at a glance.

### 7. Tabletop Challenge and Democratic Undo Voting
Honesty is the foundation of tabletop gaming. If someone accidentally taps the wrong number or an entry is challenged:
- Any player can tap the UNDO button located in the center of the board or top header.
- Unbiased Democratic Vote: The player whose turn is being challenged is barred from voting to eliminate bias. Only the other players at the table cast a YES or NO vote via on-screen buttons.
- If a majority of eligible players vote YES, the move is instantly rolled back, and the player can enter their correct roll.

### 8. Seamless Match Resume (Never Lose Progress)
Real life happens: phone calls interrupt, device screens lock, or games need to be paused for dinner. Ghost Dice continuously saves the entire game state into a local database:
- If the app is closed, swiped away, or you exit to the home screen, your match is preserved.
- When opening the app, a prominent Continue Game card appears on the home screen showing the active players, current turn, and elapsed moves.
- Tap Continue Game to jump right back into the action without losing a single move.

## The Official Rulebook of Ghost Dice

Ghost Dice adheres to the authentic international rules of Ludo while providing configurable options for custom house rules.

### 1. Starting Yards and Leaving Home
- At the start of the match, all four of each player tokens reside in their respective corner home yard.
- Tokens inside the yard are inactive and cannot move along the track.
- To move a token out of the yard onto the colored starting square, a player must roll a 6 (by default).
- Once a token reaches the starting square, it is live and can advance on subsequent turns.

### 2. Track Navigation and The Circuit
- The perimeter track consists of 52 white and colored squares arranged in a cross.
- Live tokens travel clockwise around the track by the exact number of squares indicated by the rolled die.
- After completing a full circuit around the perimeter, a token arrives back outside its colored quadrant and turns into its dedicated Home Lane.

### 3. Safe Squares (Star Zones)
Across the board, 8 squares are designated as Safe Squares:
- The 4 starting squares (one for each color).
- The 4 star-marked squares (located at the middle of each arm).
- Safe Zone Immunity: Any token resting on a safe square can never be captured by an opponent. Multiple tokens of different colors can safely occupy a star square at the same time without threat.

### 4. Capturing Opponent Tokens
- When a live token ends its move on a normal (non-star) track square already occupied by an opponent token, that opponent token is captured.
- Captured tokens are immediately knocked off the board and sent all the way back to their corner home yard.
- The player who executed the capture earns an immediate bonus turn as a reward!

### 5. Bonus Turns and Extra Rolls
A player earns an immediate extra roll under any of the following three conditions:
1. Rolling a 6: Rolling a 6 grants an extra roll.
2. Executing a Capture: Capturing an opponent token grants an extra roll.
3. Finishing a Token: Successfully moving a token into the center home triangle grants an extra roll.
Bonus rolls are executed immediately by the same player.

### 6. The Three Consecutive Sixes Penalty
While rolling a 6 is rewarding, rolling three 6s in a row triggers the classic anti-runaway rule:
- If a player rolls a 6, then another 6, and then a third 6 consecutively on the same turn, their turn is immediately forfeited.
- The third 6 cannot be used to move any piece, and the turn automatically passes to the next player. This maintains fair balance and prevents runaway leads.

### 7. Entering the Home Lane and Exact Finish
- A token entering its colored Home Lane is completely safe from all opponents; opponents can never enter another player home lane.
- The Home Lane consists of 5 colored squares leading directly into the center triangle.
- To enter the center home triangle and successfully finish a token, the player must roll the exact number required.
- If a token is 2 squares away from home, only a roll of 2 can finish it. If the player rolls a 3, 4, 5, or 6, that token cannot move and must wait for an exact roll.

## The Tactical Playbook: Team Up (2 vs 2) Rules in Detail

Team Up mode introduces deep cooperative tactics that completely transform how Ludo is played. Mastering these rules is the key to victory:

### 1. Teammate Immunity
Teammates are partners, not competitors. When your token lands on a square already occupied by your partner token:
- Neither token is captured.
- Both tokens peacefully share the square.
- Partners can travel together in tight formation to control key choke points along the track.

### 2. The Pair Shield Defense
When two tokens of the same team occupy the same square (whether they are two Red tokens, two Yellow tokens, or one Red and one Yellow token), they form a Pair Shield:
- A single enemy token landing on that square cannot capture the pair.
- Because a single token cannot capture two tokens at once, the pair is fully protected.
- The attacking enemy token simply stops on the square and waits alongside the pair, creating a high-tension standoff.

### 3. Pair vs. Pair Breaker Captures
What happens when an enemy token is waiting on a square with a shielded team pair?
- If a second enemy token lands on that same square, the defensive pair shield is broken!
- The two attacking enemy tokens capture both defending team tokens simultaneously!
- Both defending tokens are sent flying back to their respective home yards, leaving the square firmly in the control of the two attacking tokens.

### 4. Abandonment / Departure Capture (The Trap)
The pair shield only holds as long as both partner tokens remain together on the square.
- If a pair of team tokens is sharing a square with a waiting enemy token, and on their turn one of the partner tokens moves forward, they have abandoned their partner!
- The moment the first partner leaves, the remaining partner is left alone with the enemy token.
- The remaining partner token is immediately captured by the waiting enemy token and sent back to the yard!
- Exception: Departure captures do not occur if the standoff takes place on a safe star square.

### 5. Post-Finish Partner Assistance
In traditional games, when a player finishes all their tokens, they become a spectator. In Ghost Dice Team Up Mode, finishing your 4 tokens is only half the battle:
- When you finish all 4 of your personal tokens, you stay actively in the match.
- On every subsequent turn, you continue to roll your physical die.
- You use your rolled numbers to move your partner remaining tokens toward home!
- Both players roll and maneuver together until the final 8th token enters the center home triangle for the team victory!

## Strategic Tips and Advanced Tactics

Whether playing Free-For-All or Team Up 2v2, mastering the board requires foresight and calculated risks:

### 1. Early Game: Mobilize Your Fleet
- Never rely on just one live token. Whenever you roll a 6, strongly consider bringing out a second or third token from your yard.
- Having multiple active tokens gives you flexibility on future turns: you will always have a productive move regardless of what number you roll.

### 2. Mid Game: Star Staging and Ambush Trapping
- Use Safe Star Squares as staging areas. Rest your tokens on safe stars right behind high-traffic enemy territory.
- An opponent who passes your safe star is vulnerable to an ambush capture on your next roll.
- In Team Up mode, position your tokens so that your partner can easily land on your square to establish an unbreakable Pair Shield.

### 3. Threat Assessment: Offense vs. Defense
- When deciding between moving a piece forward or capturing an opponent, capturing is almost always better: it resets their progress and gives you a free bonus roll.
- However, never make a capture if it leaves your token exposed to immediate counter-capture on the very next square unless you are protected by a partner shield.

### 4. Endgame: Managing the Home Lane
- When multiple tokens are in the Home Lane, prioritize moving the tokens closest to the center home triangle.
- Freeing up the rear squares of your home lane prevents bottlenecks and ensures you can safely usher trailing tokens off the main track.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Players

### Q1: What makes Ghost Dice different from every other digital Ludo game on the store?
Most traditional digital Ludo apps use a digital random number generator (RNG) where you tap a computer button to roll a virtual die. Many players find this frustrating because computer dice often feel biased, repetitive, or rigged to favor certain outcomes. Ghost Dice brings back true tabletop authenticity by letting you use your own real physical dice. You roll on the table in front of your friends, and the app simply serves as the intelligent, automated board.

### Q2: Do I need any special Bluetooth or smart dice to play?
No! You can use any standard six-sided die (D6) that you have at home—from a board game box, a tabletop roleplaying set, or a toy store. There is no expensive hardware or electronic sensor required. You simply look at what you rolled and tap that number on the screen.

### Q3: What if we do not have a physical die with us?
No problem at all! While Ghost Dice is designed for real physical dice, you can also tap the active player corner dock to roll a virtual die directly within the app whenever you are traveling without dice.

### Q4: How does Ghost Dice prevent players from cheating when entering their numbers?
Ghost Dice is designed for friends and families sitting together in the same room. Because all players are looking at the table when the real die is rolled, everyone naturally sees the number. Additionally, Ghost Dice incorporates two unique fairness mechanisms:
1. 3-Second Center Roll Popup: Every time a number is entered, the number pops up prominently in the center of the board with the player name so everyone at the table sees exactly what was entered.
2. Tabletop Democratic Undo Voting: If a player enters the wrong number—whether by accident or dispute—any player can hit the Undo button. A fair majority vote among the other players immediately rolls back the move so the correct roll can be played.

### Q5: Can 2 or 3 players play, or do we always need 4 players?
Ghost Dice supports 2, 3, or 4 players! In Classic Mode, you can choose any number of players from 2 to 4. In Team Up Mode, all 4 color quadrants are used for the 2 vs 2 alliance battle (Red & Yellow vs Green & Blue). If playing Team Up with two people, each person controls two allied colors, making for an intense head-to-head tactical showdown!

### Q6: Can teammates capture each other tokens in Team Up Mode?
Never! Teammates are allies working together. Landing on a partner square does not capture their piece; instead, both tokens share the square and create a defensive Pair Shield that enemy tokens cannot capture alone.

### Q7: What happens if someone leaves a game mid-match?
Ghost Dice automatically saves every single move to local storage. You can exit to the home screen or close the app entirely. Whenever you reopen the app, tap Continue Game on the home screen to instantly resume the match right where you left off.

## Board Geography: Understanding the 52-Square Circuit

A standard Ludo board is built with mathematical precision, and understanding its geography will make you a formidable competitor:

- The Cross Track (52 Squares): The main highway of the game. Every piece must travel along this 52-square loop before reaching safety.
- Starting Squares: Squares 1 (Red), 14 (Green), 27 (Yellow), and 40 (Blue) are starting squares. When exiting your yard, your token lands on your starting square. Starting squares are permanent Safe Zones.
- Star Squares: Located at squares 9, 22, 35, and 48. These are the critical waystations along the track where tokens cannot be captured.
- The Turn-Off Points: Squares 51, 12, 25, and 38 are the critical threshold squares. Once a token reaches this point after completing a full loop, it leaves the dangerous outer highway and turns inward into its colored Home Lane.
- The Home Lane (5 Squares): A safe colored corridor exclusive to your tokens. No opponent can ever enter or threaten your pieces in this lane.
- The Center Home Triangle: The final destination! Maneuver all 4 of your tokens here to secure your triumph.
