# Ghost Dice 🎲👻

**Physical Dice. Digital Board.**

Ghost Dice is a tabletop companion and automated digital Ludo board designed for 2 to 4 players gathering around a single shared device. Instead of relying on a random number generator on a screen, players roll **real, physical tabletop dice** and enter their rolled numbers into the app. Ghost Dice handles all the board calculations, token movement, captures, team synergies, and fair gameplay rules!

---

## 🌟 About Ghost Dice

Traditional board games are best when you can feel the dice in your hand, but keeping track of complex rules, captures, and token movements can slow down the action. Ghost Dice combines the best of both worlds:
- **Real Tabletop Feel**: Roll your favorite physical dice on the table.
- **Automated Board & Rules**: The app computes legal moves, moves tokens along the track, handles safe squares, and tracks scores.
- **Fairness & Transparency**: Public roll popups and democratic challenge voting keep every player honest.
- **No Internet or Accounts Required**: Pure local pass-and-play fun for game nights, friends, and family.

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 1. Classic Mode (Free-For-All)
- For 2 to 4 players.
- Every player competes independently with their own set of 4 tokens (Red, Green, Yellow, or Blue).
- The first player to successfully navigate all 4 tokens around the board and into the center home triangle wins the game!

### 2. Team Up Mode (2 vs 2 Co-op)
- Two allied teams battle for tabletop dominance:
  - **Team 1**: Red & Yellow
  - **Team 2**: Green & Blue
- Teammates cooperate strategically using shared defensive shields and team captures.
- **Team Victory**: A team only wins when **all 8 tokens** across both teammates reach home!

## 📱 How Everything Works in the App

### 1. Match Setup
- Select **Classic** or **Team Up (2 vs 2)** from the home screen.
- Customize player names and review match rule toggles before entering the arena.
- Each player is seated at an authentic corner dock displaying their token avatar, name, and personal dice box.

### 2. Taking Your Turn & Rolling
- The active player’s corner tag glows gold with an animated indicator pointing to their dice slot.
- **Roll your physical die** on the table.
- **Tap the rolled number (1 to 6)** on the bottom dice selection dock.
- *(Optional)*: Tapping the active player's corner dice box can also trigger a roll.

### 3. Moving Tokens
- Once a number is entered, the app highlights all tokens that can legally make a move.
- Tap any highlighted token to send it hopping along the track with smooth 3D jump animations and sound effects.
- **Smart Auto-Play**: If only a single token can legally move, the app automatically moves it for you, keeping the pace fast and seamless.

### 4. 3-Second Center Roll Display (Fairness Pop-up)
- Whenever a player selects a number and moves a piece, a large 3D die displaying the chosen value pops up in the **center home box** of the board for **3 seconds**.
- This ensures all players sitting around the table can clearly see, verify, and agree on what number was entered.

### 5. Tabletop Undo & Democratic Voting System
Ghost Dice is built on mutual trust and provides a built-in democratic voting system for mistakes:
- If someone accidentally taps the wrong number or an entry is contested, any player can tap the **UNDO** button in the center of the board.
- **Fair Voting**: The player whose move is challenged cannot vote to eliminate bias. The remaining players vote **YES** or **NO**.
- If the majority votes YES, the move is instantly rolled back to the previous turn state so the correct roll can be entered.

### 6. Seamless Game Resume
- Never lose match progress! If the app is closed, minimized, or you exit to the home screen, your match is automatically saved.
- When opening the app, a **Continue Game** banner lets you resume right where you left off.

## 📜 Official Game Rules

### Entering the Board (Leaving the Yard)
- All tokens start inside their colored home yard.
- A player must roll a **6** to bring a token out of the yard onto their starting square (this can be toggled in settings to allow any roll).

### Track Navigation & Home Run
- Tokens travel clockwise around the perimeter track (52 squares total).
- After completing a full circuit, tokens turn into their colored **Home Lane** towards the center triangle.

### Safe Squares (★)
- Squares marked with a **Star (★)** and player starting squares are **Safe Zones**.
- Tokens on safe squares can never be captured. Multiple tokens of different colors can safely share a star square.

### Captures
- Landing on an opponent’s token on a regular track square **captures** it and sends it back to its starting yard.
- Capturing an opponent grants an **extra bonus roll**!

### Bonus Rolls
Players earn an immediate extra roll in three situations:
1. Rolling a **6**.
2. Capturing an opponent's token.
3. Successfully moving a token into the center home.

### Three Sixes Forfeit Rule
- Rolling three consecutive 6s immediately forfeits the turn to prevent runaway luck. The third roll is discarded, and play passes to the next player.

### Exact Roll to Finish
- Tokens must reach the center triangle with an **exact roll**. If the number rolled is higher than the remaining steps, the token cannot move and must wait for the exact number.

## 🤝 Team Up (2 vs 2) Special Rules

Team Up mode introduces cooperative tactical mechanics:

1. **Teammates Never Capture Each Other**:
   - Landing on a square occupied by your partner does not capture their token. Both partner tokens share the square peacefully.

2. **The Pair Shield Rule**:
   - When two tokens of the same team occupy the same square, they form a **defensive shield**.
   - A single opponent entering that square **cannot capture** them. The opponent simply waits on the square alongside the pair.

3. **Pair vs. Pair Capture**:
   - If two team tokens are sharing a square with a waiting opponent, and a **second opponent token** arrives, the defensive pair is broken!
   - Both defending tokens are captured simultaneously and sent home.

4. **Abandonment / Departure Capture**:
   - If two team tokens are shielding each other on a square with a waiting opponent, and one teammate moves away on their turn, they abandon their partner!
   - The remaining partner token is immediately captured by the waiting opponent (unless on a safe star square).

5. **Post-Finish Partner Play**:
   - When one teammate finishes all 4 of their tokens, they stay in the game! On their turn, they continue rolling their dice and move their **partner's remaining tokens** until all 8 team tokens reach home!

## ⚙️ House Rules & Customization

Before starting any match, hosts can toggle rules to suit their house preferences:
- **Need a 6 to enter the board**: Require a 6 or allow any roll to leave the yard.
- **Three 6s forfeits turn**: Toggle the three-sixes forfeiture rule.
- **Exact roll to reach home**: Require exact rolls or allow any roll equal/higher.
- **Two pieces form a blockade**: Enable or disable same-color blockades.
- **Starred squares are safe**: Toggle star safe-zone immunity.
